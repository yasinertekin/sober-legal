Getting started

Your first day starts with Ponu asking about yesterday. Then you pick today’s number on your week, spend your first coins on the spring, and find where Pause, your savings and your notebook live.

  • Week 0 is the time before your plan’s first week. It has no goal yet, so the week card shows your usual week beside what you log, and the spring never goes quiet.
  • Pause, Saved and Notebook sit in one row under the week card on Home. Saved appears once you set a price per drink.
  • The day turns at 04:00. A drink at 1 a.m. still belongs to the evening before.
  • The morning reminder comes at 10:00 with today’s settings. Settings › Preferences has its switch and a row to change the time. It doesn’t come on a morning you have already checked in. It pauses while you have no subscription or free week and comes back after a purchase or restore.
  • The Home Screen widget: Settings › Home Screen widget shows how to add it.
  • Language: Settings › Preferences › Language. Ponu speaks eleven languages and follows your iPhone unless you pick one.

Mornings with Ponu

Every morning starts with yesterday. Tell Ponu what you drank, or save the day as alcohol-free. That is a check-in.

  • A cocktail counts as 2 drinks.
  • Tap + at any time to note today’s drinks so far. You confirm them at your next morning check-in.

Today’s number is how many drinks you choose for the day. Press and hold the button to set it. A number you set in the evening returns at your next morning check-in as last night’s number: keep it or change it.

With today’s settings:

  • a number set before 17:00 is for today, and from 17:00 it is for tomorrow;
  • a number counts as kept when the day ends at it or up to 20 % above it, so a number of 5 allows 6;
  • a kept number pays 40 coins. A number of 8 or more pays none, and Ponu shows a note about seeing a doctor. A day with 8 or more drinks logged pays none either, even when the number was kept.

The morning spin

Logging yesterday, or confirming yesterday’s + entry, earns that morning’s spin. A + entry on its own, a filled-in older day or an edited day earns none.

  • Your first spin is always the same costume.
  • Until your first week ends, the wheel gives only costumes Ponu doesn’t have yet.
  • After that, the Odds button on the wheel shows your chances. With today’s settings each of the eight slices is 12.5 %: seven costumes, the ones Ponu doesn’t have yet first, and one coin slice that pays 40 coins. A costume Ponu already has pays nothing.
  • Spins can’t be bought. They come only from check-ins. Coins have no cash value either.
  • Costumes are worn automatically.

Looking after the spring

The spring has seven things to make nicer with coins.

  • A day with no check-in makes the spring quiet: the steam thins, the water clouds over and a few leaves drift in. Nothing breaks and nothing costs coins. Your next check-in brings the sparkle back.
  • Tap the coin count at the top of Home. Ponu spends your coins on upgrades, cheapest first, as far as they reach. Each price shows on its object.
  • Coins come from the first day, kept numbers, finished weeks and the wheel. They have no cash value and can’t be bought, sold or exchanged.
  • The map shows the river and Ponu’s place on it.

How the plan moves

Gentle, Brisk or Quick decides how many weeks lie between your usual week and your goal. Your weeks, behind the arrow on the week card, shows every week as a bar beside its goal.

  • A week closes on the calendar. Days with no check-in count as 0.
  • After a week above its goal, Ponu draws the next weeks again from that week’s total. None gets stricter than in your first plan.
  • To change the plan, use Settings › Your usual week or Settings › My goal. Past weeks keep their score; a change counts from this week.

With today’s settings, a closed week pays coins by where it ended:

The week endedCoins
Below the week’s goal150
At the week’s goal75
Above the goal, not above your usual week40
Above your usual week0, and no chest

The morning after a week closes, Ponu shows a recap and, unless the week ended above your usual week, a chest to open.

Start over (Settings › Start over) begins a new plan, and today becomes Week 1. Check-ins and numbers, weeks and coins, the spring, costumes and spins start fresh, and your first day starts again. Your subscription, your notes, your name, your settings and your price stay. Ponu asks for your usual week and your goal again.

Changing a past day

  • Tap a day on the week card, or open History from Your weeks, and change what you logged for it.
  • A change to a past day pays no coins, and it does not score that day’s number or its week again.
  • To take a day back, set it to 0. Days are never deleted.

How paying works

  • The free week. When the yearly plan offers it, you start with a free week. The monthly plan has none. If notifications are on, Ponu reminds you three days before the free week ends.
  • Cancel or change. Settings › Profile › Manage subscription opens your Apple ID subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, or it renews.
  • A new phone or a reinstall. Settings › Profile › Restore purchases brings your subscription back. Every subscription screen has a Restore button too.
  • Refunds. Apple handles payments and refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • No account. Ponu has no sign-in. What you log stays on this iPhone.
  • A new phone. Restoring your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings Ponu’s data along. A fresh install starts over; Restore purchases brings your subscription back.
  • Export my data (Settings › Profile) gives you your data as files.
  • Delete data on this phone (Settings › Profile) erases what you entered and your progress. A few device settings stay, such as a no to usage data. It does not cancel your subscription.
  • Usage data. Ponu asks before sending any. Its switch is in Settings › Preferences. Usage statistics never include what you log. Crash reports are always sent; they never include what you log either.
If you need help now

If you or someone else is in danger, dial the emergency number where you are: 112 in Türkiye and across the EU, 911 in the US, 999 or 112 in the UK.

To find a helpline where you live, see findahelpline.com.

Ponu is not medical care. If you drink heavily most days, stopping suddenly can be dangerous, so talk to a doctor first.
