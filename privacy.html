Ponu: Capybara Drink Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026 · Türkçe

Ponu is an iPhone app made by Yasin Ertekin ("I", "me"), an independent developer in Türkiye. I am the data controller for the information described here.

The short version
  • What you log stays on your iPhone. Ponu has no account and keeps no copy on a server.
  • Usage statistics go to Google Firebase only if you say yes. Crash reports always go there, so I can fix bugs. Neither includes what you log.
  • No ads, no tracking, and I don't sell your information.
  • Apple handles payments. Adapty runs the subscription and receives technical and purchase information, not what you log.
  • You can export your data and delete it in the app.

What you put into Ponu

Ponu saves what you enter on your iPhone: check-ins, goals, plans and daily numbers, your setup answers, your reasons and notes, Pause sessions, savings settings, the name Ponu calls you, reminder settings, and your coins and rewards.

I receive none of it unless you send it to me yourself. Export and sharing happen only when you start them. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS includes Ponu's data in those backups under your own Apple settings.

No account, no cloud backup

Ponu has no sign-in and keeps no copy of your data on a server. Restoring your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings Ponu's data along; Restore purchases brings your subscription back.

Usage statistics and crash reports

Ponu uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to learn which screens work and why the app crashes.

  • Usage statistics: Ponu asks first. Nothing is sent before you answer, and nothing if you say no. Change your answer any time with Share usage data in Settings › Preferences. Turning it off stops usage statistics and remote settings and resets their ID on your iPhone.
  • Crash reports are always on, so I can find and fix bugs. They never include what you log. The usage events just before a crash are added only if you said yes.
  • Under 18: answering Under 18 ends setup and turns usage statistics and crash reports off.

With a yes, Firebase receives usage events (such as a setup step, a saved check-in, a spin or a purchase with its product and price), a few labels (app language, build, plan week in broad ranges, whether sounds and the widget are on) and your approximate location (country and region), worked out from your network address. Always, it receives crash reports (what the app was doing, error types, device model, iOS and app version, free memory), session start times and a random installation ID.

Firebase never receives what or how much you drink, your numbers, targets or goal, setup answers, gender or age, notes, names or the drink price you set. Advertising features are off, and Ponu gives Firebase neither the advertising identifier nor the identifier for vendor (IDFV). Delete data on this phone deletes the installation ID.

Remote settings and the time check

Only after your yes to usage data, Ponu fetches remote settings from Firebase Remote Config (numbers such as rewards and timings), and asks a Ponu server function for the current time, so that rewards follow the real date. Remote Config receives the installation ID, country, language, time zone, iOS and app version, the labels above and when the app was first opened. The time check sends nothing about you; Google's logs see your network address. Firebase App Check protects the time check with Apple's App Attest, to confirm requests come from the real Ponu app.

Subscriptions

Ponu Premium is an auto-renewable subscription. Apple handles purchase, billing and cancellation; I never receive your payment details.

Ponu uses Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc., United States) to show the subscription screens and check your subscription. From the first launch, whatever you answer about usage data, Adapty receives:

  • an identifier created for this app, and the identifier for vendor (IDFV);
  • device model, screen size, iOS and app version, language, time zone, App Store country, install date and app opens;
  • which subscription screen was shown, and your App Store purchases and restores;
  • once per installation, Apple's ad attribution token, so Adapty can learn from Apple which Apple Search Ads campaign, if any, led to the installation.

Adapty never receives anything you log. Ponu connects no ad network to Adapty, doesn't let it collect the advertising identifier, and doesn't track you across other companies' apps. Adapty's policy: adapty.io/privacy.

Notifications

Ponu's reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, only if you allow them. They never mention alcohol or amounts. Turn them off in Settings › Preferences or in iOS Settings.

Widget

The Home Screen widget reads a small file that only Ponu and its widget can open. It holds what the widget shows for the day, never what you drank, and neither Ponu nor the widget sends it anywhere. Anyone who sees your Home Screen sees the widget.

Where your data goes

Google and Adapty process data for me under their data processing terms. Apple, and Microsoft for email, act under their own policies. I don't sell your information.

Your choices

  • Usage statistics: the Share usage data switch in Settings › Preferences.
  • Export: Export my data in Settings › Profile gives you your data as files (JSON and CSV).
  • Delete data on this phone (Settings › Profile; Delete all local data on the subscription screen) erases what you entered, your progress and your settings. A few device settings stay, such as a no to usage data. Your subscription is not touched.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel it in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Deleting your data does not cancel it.

Legal bases

  • Subscription (Apple, Adapty): to provide the service you signed up for. GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c).
  • Usage statistics and remote settings: your consent. GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1). The switch withdraws it.
  • Crash reports, subscription statistics and ad attribution, security (Crashlytics, Adapty, App Check, request logs): my legitimate interest in a reliable, safe app and in knowing which subscription screens and campaigns work. GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f).
  • Your requests and legal duties: GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (ç).

Transfers to other countries

I'm based in Türkiye. The time check runs in the EU (europe-west1). Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config and App Check run on Google's servers in the United States and other countries; Adapty runs in the United States. These transfers rely on the standard contractual clauses in Google's and Adapty's data processing terms. For users in Türkiye, sending this data to Google and Adapty is a transfer abroad in the sense of Article 9 of KVKK. You can ask me about the transfer or for a copy of the clauses.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, for a copy, and to correct, delete, restrict or move it. You can withdraw consent at any time. I answer within one month (30 days under KVKK), free of charge. Ponu makes no automated decisions with legal or similar effects about you.

Your right to object: you can object to anything based on my legitimate interest by writing to me. Crash reports have no switch in the app; deleting the app stops them.

You can also complain to your data protection authority: in the EU see the list of authorities; in the UK the ICO; in Switzerland the FDPIC; in Türkiye the KVKK Board.

Health and age

Ponu is for adults aged 18 and over. I don't knowingly process children's data; if you think a child's data reached me, write to me and I'll delete it. Ponu helps you track and reflect; it is not medical care.

Security
