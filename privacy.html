Sober: Alcohol Moderation Pet

Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Türkçe

Sober is an iPhone app made by Yasin Ertekin ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Sober.

The short version
  • There is no account. What you log in Sober stays on your iPhone, unless you choose to share it.
  • I can't see your check-ins, goals, notes or anything else you enter.
  • No ads and no tracking. Sober has no analytics service of its own.
  • Subscriptions go through Apple. The subscription service Adapty receives technical and purchase information to run them, but nothing you log.

What you enter stays on your iPhone

While you use Sober you may enter the name Sober calls you, your goal (cutting back, staying alcohol-free or taking a break), your weekly limit, alcohol-free-day target or break length, your usual weekly amount, your reasons, your hardest weekdays, daily check-ins, weekly plans and heads-up days with their notes, Pause with Sober sessions (what prompted them and how they ended), notes and reflections, savings settings, reminder times and app preferences such as appearance, haptics and language. Sober's journey, postcards, coins and wardrobe are stored the same way.

All of this is saved only in the app's storage on your device. It is not sent to me or to anyone else, and the app does not sync it to iCloud. It leaves your iPhone only if you choose to share it yourself, for example with Export or by sharing a postcard. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS includes app data in those backups under your own Apple settings.

The subscription screen can show your name, your goal and plan (such as your weekly limit, alcohol-free days or break length), how it compares with your usual week, a line based on your reasons, and dates. That text is put together on your iPhone and is not sent anywhere.

Subscriptions

Sober Premium is an auto-renewable subscription. Apple handles the purchase, billing and cancellation through your Apple Account; I never receive your payment details. The app asks Apple's App Store for prices, free-trial eligibility, purchases and restores.

To show the subscription screens and know whether your subscription is active, Sober uses Adapty, a subscription management service. Adapty receives:

  • an identifier created for this app, and Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV);
  • technical information: device model, screen size, iOS and app version, language, time zone, App Store country, and when the app was installed and how often it is opened;
  • subscription-related usage: app opens, which subscription screen was shown, and purchases and restores;
  • your App Store purchase and subscription records.

Sober tells Adapty's software not to collect the advertising identifier or look up your IP address, and Adapty's attribution features are off. Like any server, Adapty sees the network address a request comes from. Adapty does not receive anything you log in Sober. Adapty keeps this subscription record to provide its service; to ask for it to be removed, write to me. Adapty's own policy: adapty.io/privacy.

Notifications

Sober's notifications are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone, only if you allow them: daily and weekly reminders, nudges on the heads-up days you mark in your plan, a follow-up after a Pause, and a reminder before a free trial ends. Their text shows the app's name, Sober, but never mentions alcohol, amounts or your plans. Daily and weekly reminders can be turned off in Sober's Settings; all of Sober's notifications can be turned off in iOS Settings.

What I don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data brokers.
  • No crash reporting, and no analytics service besides the subscription events Adapty records.
  • I don't sell your information or share it with anyone except Apple and Adapty as described above.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings in Sober lets you export your data as files (JSON and CSV).
  • Delete: Settings in Sober lets you delete everything you entered from this iPhone. Deleting the app also removes it. Your anonymous subscription record at Adapty is not affected; write to me to have it removed.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting your data in Sober does not cancel a subscription.

Health and age

Sober is for adults aged 18 and over. It helps you track and reflect; it is not medical care or treatment. Because what you log stays on your device, I do not process health information on any server.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example before any new service is added to the app, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
