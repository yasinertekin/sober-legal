Support

Questions, a problem with the app, or an idea for Sober? Write to yasinertekinn@outlook.com. I read every message.

Subscriptions

  • Cancel or change your plan: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sober.
  • Restore a purchase on a new iPhone: open Sober and tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen or in Settings.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Everything you log stays on your iPhone. You can export it or delete it in Sober's Settings. See the Privacy Policy.

Looking for help with drinking?

Sober is a companion for your own goals, not medical care. If you drink heavily most days, stopping suddenly can be dangerous: please talk to a doctor before you stop or cut down a lot. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number.
